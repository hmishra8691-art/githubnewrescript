Intelligent copilot: research-aware survey design and programming through a controlled action layer

Intelligent mode becomes an AI research and survey-programming copilot on the
existing AI integration. A researcher types or speaks (any language, Hinglish
included) an objective, a hypothesis, research documents or a change; the model
reasons about the research - objective, hypotheses, population, independent /
dependent / mediating / control variables, method, analysis - and programs the
survey through a closed action vocabulary in the engine (create block /
question / branch / loop / randomizer / calculation / quota / embedded data,
update, move, delete, display logic, skips, validation, page breaks, the
research design). Nothing the model writes is executed directly: the engine
applies every action to a clone - references resolved against the real survey,
conditions through the expression parser, questions made by the picker's own
maker (moved into the engine) - refuses what does not resolve with the reason,
flags what removes or rewrites existing content, and diffs the result.

The workspace has the survey on the left, the conversation in the centre and
a panel on the right: Changes (exactly what will happen, destructive changes
behind a confirmation, refused actions, new problems, before/after structure,
field-by-field changes), Review (engine checks - logic, reachability, ranges,
scales, duplicates, wording, length, hypothesis coverage - merged with the
model's reading, grouped Critical / Warning / Suggestion, fixes as previews),
Research (uploaded PDFs - scanned pages read by the vision model - Word, text,
CSV, Excel; each summarised once into a cited research card), History (AI
Change #001..., each undoable as one operation). A follow-up while a proposal
is open revises it; Apply is one labelled undoable edit, audited. Context is
kept minimal: a bounded outline with the named questions in full, research
passages only for research requests (BM25, embeddings when configured),
the model's own running memory, a short response cache. Every model call is
metered. The grammar remains for exact read-only questions and when no model
is configured.
