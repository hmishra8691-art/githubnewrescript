# Study methodologies catalogue

**Study design** recommends a methodology from your research objective and builds it as a complete, programmed block of questions with its analysis plan. The table below is generated from the same registry the Studio uses, so it always lists exactly what is available.

## How to use one

::: steps Build a study from a methodology
1. **Open Research Tools → Study design** — or pick a study method in Design Generators' library and open its sample.
2. **Describe the objective** (typed or by voice) — e.g. "how much would customers pay for the new kettle?" — and press **Recommend a methodology**; the recommendation explains why, with alternatives.
3. **Fill the method's inputs** — product, prices, concepts, brands, features or attributes.
4. **Add it to the survey** — the questions, randomization and analysis-plan items are created through the same checked actions.
:::

## Common mistakes

- **Choosing by name instead of by objective** — describe the decision the research informs; Study design says when a named method does not fit.

## Troubleshooting

- *A method needs inputs you do not have yet* — the sample inputs show the shape; replace them before fielding.
