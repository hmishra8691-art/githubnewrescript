## What it does
In Intelligent Mode you describe what you want — “add a screener for car owners aged 18–65”, “show Q5 only to people who said Yes at Q2”, “check the logic” — and ResLens.ai proposes the change. Nothing is applied until you review and approve it, and every AI change can be undone as one operation.

## What it can do automatically
Draft a whole questionnaire from an objective or hypothesis, add and rewrite questions, write display/skip logic, quotas, calculations, masks and loops, set up design generators and methodology templates, review the survey (logic, reachability, wording, scales, duplicates, length), repair what the review finds, simulate test respondents, answer questions about your data, and import questionnaires and quota sheets.

## What needs you
Approving each proposal; anything ambiguous (it asks); choices that are research judgements (target sample, which method fits — it recommends and explains); and anything outside the survey definition, such as payments or inviting collaborators.

## How to set it up
1. Open the **Mode** menu and choose **Intelligent**.
2. Type what you want in the input and press **Propose**.
3. Read the proposal — what it understood and the exact change — then **Apply**, or say how to revise it.
4. Use **Review** to have the survey checked, **Generate survey** to draft from an objective, and **Undo AI change** to roll back the last AI change.
5. Attach research documents, a questionnaire to import or a quota sheet with **Attach files**.

## Cost
AI requests are charged to the project's wallet (or not charged when you use your own connected AI provider). See “How the usage wallet works”.

## Common mistakes
- Very long multi-part instructions — give one goal per request, or use **Plan first** for big restructurings.
- Approving without reading the proposal: the proposal lists exactly what will change.
