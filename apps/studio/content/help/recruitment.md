## What it does
**Recruitment** plans and buys sample: you say what you need, it checks feasibility across recruitment partners, compares cost and capacity, and tracks orders and delivery.

## How to set it up
1. Open **Results** → **Recruitment**.
2. Under **What do you need?** enter **Completes needed**, **Survey length (min)**, **Expected incidence (%)**, **Timeline (days)** and the markets with completes per market (**Add market**).
3. Click **Check feasibility** to see the **Recruitment plan** and the **Recruitment partner comparison** (capacity, cost per valid complete, expected days).
4. Follow **Recruitment progress** — accepted, delivered, remaining, budget, quality — by market and quota cell.

## Notes
Partners and their pricing are configured by platform administrators; you see the plan and the comparison.
