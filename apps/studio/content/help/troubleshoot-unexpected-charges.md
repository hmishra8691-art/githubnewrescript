## What to check
1. **Management** → **Usage & Wallet** → **Recent usage**: every charge is listed with its operation, amount and **Balance after**. **Usage by category** shows which kind of activity it was (AI, translation, responses, storage…).
2. AI work — Intelligent Mode, AI writing, translation, Help answers written by the model — is charged per request on the project it was done for.
3. Whether test activity is charged depends on your installation's setting (Help's billing answers state it).
4. Your own connected AI provider: if AI requests were charged, check the project is set to use your provider.

## How to fix
Set a **Spending limit** on the project to cap spending. If a charge looks wrong after checking Recent usage, ask your administrator — administrators can reverse a usage event, and Help cannot change billing.
