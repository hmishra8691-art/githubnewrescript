## What it does
**Survey Settings** holds the survey-wide choices: title and code, the survey URL, who can take it (open link, password, unique links, email invitations), whether respondents may retake it, dictation of open answers, the research design, and the quality checks.

## How to set it up
1. Open **Programming** → **Survey Settings**.
2. Set the **Title**, **Survey code** and **Survey URL** (with **Client slug** and **Study slug**, and an optional custom domain).
3. Choose **Who can take this survey** → **Access mode**: **Open link — anyone with the URL**, **Password protected**, **Unique respondent links** or **Email invitations**.
4. Decide on **Allow a respondent to retake the survey** and **Let respondents dictate open-text answers (microphone)**.
5. Further down, **Quality checks** turns the quality engine on and sets its strictness and rules.
6. Changes reach respondents when you save a version and publish it.
