## What it does
Each project holds one survey with its data, files and team. From **Projects** you open, clone, set the status of, cap the spending of or delete a project; inside it, **Management** holds its settings, wallet, team and history.

## How to set it up
On **Projects** (each project card's menu):
- **Clone project…** — a copy of the survey to start from (responses are not copied).
- **Set status**, **Spending limit…**, **Collaborators…**, **Delete survey…**.

Inside a project:
1. **Management** → **Project**: who it is for, dates, notes, the collaboration rules, and **Freeze this project.** (owner only) to stop all changes.
2. **Management** → **Activity**: who changed what, and when.
3. **Management** → **Usage & Wallet**: what the project has spent.
4. **Management** → **Internal notes**: notes for the team, never shown to respondents.

## Project vs organisation
Project settings apply to this project. Workspace policies (AI connections, who may share, billing configuration) are set by administrators for the whole organisation.
