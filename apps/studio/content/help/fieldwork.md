## What it does
**Fieldwork** shows how collection is going — completes against target, starts, in progress, screened, quota full, incidence, completion rate and median time — overall and by supplier, plus your declared sample sources.

## How to set it up
1. Open **Results** → **Fieldwork**.
2. **By supplier** breaks the numbers down by source; **Declared sources** lists them with codes, completes target and cost per complete. Add one with **+ Declare source** — each source gets its own link parameter.
3. Watch quota progress on the **Quota Dashboard** (Programming → Quotas).

## Common mistakes
Averaging incidence across suppliers — rates are not summed; the overall row is computed from the totals.
