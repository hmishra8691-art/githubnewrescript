## What it does
**Branding** controls how the survey looks to respondents — colours, fonts, logo, backgrounds, card and option styles, progress bar, button labels — and can derive a palette for you, with every text colour checked for readable contrast.

## How to set it up
1. Open **Research Tools** → **Branding**.
2. Start from a palette: **From your website** (it reads the site's colours and fonts), **From a document or your logo** (a **Brand guidelines (PDF)** or your logo), or **From one brand colour** — then **Use this palette**. Or pick a theme under **Start from**.
3. Fine-tune: colours for buttons, page, cards, text and errors; **Font family** and **Base size**; **Card style**; **Progress bar** position; logo size and fit; **Next label** and **Back** label.
4. For anything else, add custom CSS in the advanced section.
5. To reuse a house style across projects, use **Apply workspace theme…**.
6. Check the result in the live preview beside the settings, then on a phone with **Preview**.

## Common mistakes
- Low-contrast colours — the palette checks readability; keep its warnings at zero.
- Large background images on mobile: test on a phone.

## Reports
Analytics reports and presentations use report themes (Data Analytics → Report themes), set separately from the survey's look.
