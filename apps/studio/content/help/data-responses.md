## What it does
**Data** lists every response row by row — test and live separately — with status (complete, in progress, screened, quota full, terminated), quality class and the answers flattened into your variables. From here you manage responses and export them.

## How to set it up
1. Open **Results** → **Data** (or **Data** in the top bar).
2. **Responses** lists them; open one to see its answers. **Manage** imports, edits or removes responses (removals are soft and can be restored). **Quality** shows each response's quality verdict.
3. Choose the **Dataset for table & exports:** — **All responses (removed included)**, **Clean dataset — approved + unreviewed CLEAN; removed out**, or **Custom — exclude selected classifications**.
4. Export as CSV, Excel, SPSS, SAS or Stata; tick **Include the data dictionary** or timing columns, and save the settings as an export preset (**Save preset**).

## Common mistakes
- Looking at the live dataset for test responses (or the reverse).
- Exporting the full dataset when the clean one was intended.
