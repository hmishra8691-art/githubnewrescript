## What it does
**Study design** turns your objective into a recommended methodology — with why it fits, the alternatives, the strengths and trade-offs, the sample you need and the analysis it supports — and can build the questions, design file, analysis plan and a simulation for you to review.

## How to set it up
1. Open **Research Tools** → **Study design**.
2. Describe the objective in your own words (for example “We are launching a premium subscription and need to know how much customers would pay”) and click **Recommend a methodology**.
3. Read the recommendation, the alternatives and what is **Still needed:**.
4. Fill in the inputs, click **Build and check the study**, review the preview (questions, **Analysis plan**, **Simulation**), then **Add to the survey**.

## In Intelligent Mode
Ask “Which method should I use to …?” — the same recommendation appears in the conversation.
