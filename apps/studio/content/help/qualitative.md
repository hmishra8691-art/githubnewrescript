## What it does
**Qualitative** covers the whole qual study: a plan from your research context, the interview or focus-group guide, AI-moderated interviews (voice, chat or both), recorded sessions and transcripts, coding (by hand and AI-proposed, with review), themes, synthesis and the report.

## How to set it up
1. Open **Research Tools** → **Qualitative**.
2. **Plan**: under **Describe your research**, explain what you want to learn, why, and who you want to hear from. The plan proposes objectives, themes with depth, questions and probes, the interview format and **Interview length (minutes)**, and asks about anything a good brief still lacks.
3. Edit the plan — **+ Theme**, **+ Question**, probes, **Closing question**, what respondents see first (purpose, consent, recording disclosure) — then **Approve and create the study**.
4. **AI interviews**: set **Format**, **Probing**, **Max follow-ups per question**, **Moderator name**, **Tone**, **Interviews wanted**; try it with **Start a simulated interview** or **Test as respondent**; then **Launch — create the participant link**.
5. **Sessions** and **Transcripts**: record or import sessions, transcribe recordings, and manage speakers.
6. **Coding**, **Themes**, **Synthesis**: code passages, accept or reject AI-proposed codes, build themes, and draw insights; **Report** assembles the findings.

## Settings explained
- **Probing** and **Max follow-ups per question** — how deep the AI moderator goes before moving on.
- **Interview length (minutes)** — the plan's estimate from the number of themes and their depth; keep it realistic for the format.
- **Unexpected topics** — whether the moderator may follow a relevant topic outside the guide.

## Common mistakes
- A guide with too many themes for the length — depth suffers. Cut themes rather than probes.
- Launching without a simulated interview — always try it first.
