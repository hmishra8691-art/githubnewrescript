## What works
- **Name the questions** by their codes when you can: “Show Q5 only if Q2 = Yes” is unambiguous.
- **Say the outcome, not the mechanism**: “People who don't own a car should not see the car questions” — Intelligent Mode picks display logic, skip logic or a branch.
- **One goal per request.** Several unrelated changes in one sentence are harder to review.
- **For a new survey, give a brief**: the objective, who you want to hear from, the decisions the data will inform, the length you can afford, and any must-ask questions. Attach a brief or past report if you have one.
- **Ask for a review before fielding**: “Review the survey” checks logic, reachability, wording, scales and length.
- **Ask questions too**: “Which method should I use to price the premium plan?” gets a methodology recommendation with its reasoning.

## Examples
- “Add a quota of 50% men and 50% women, hard, terminate when full.”
- “Carry forward the brands selected at Q3 into Q4 and keep None of these.”
- “Generate 100 test responses and test every path.”
