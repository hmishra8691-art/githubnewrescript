# Research Engine — Phase 6: The research agent (09-10-2026)

**Source:** the Research Engine audit (`RESEARCH-ENGINE-AUDIT-2026-10-08.md`, section K, Phase 6; the execution router ⑩ of section F), on top of Phases 1–5. The outcome it set: *the Research Engine as an operating system the researcher directs in plain language and approves step by step.* Every change has a regression test and the tests were mutation-checked. Nothing existing was removed: the copilot's change plan, the Changes review, History and its rollback, the output route and the Findings tab are as they were; the agent sits on top of them and uses them — a workflow step is a proposal like any other, a document is the Phase 5 output, a model step is a model turn.

## What was built

**The planner (`packages/engine/src/researchPlanner.ts`)** — `researchWorkflow` builds the steps in order, each from the survey and the steps before it: a step is blocked by the first unmet prerequisite (the hypotheses by the objective, the framework by the hypotheses, the questionnaire by the framework, the recommendations by the plan, the deck by the plan and by fieldwork data). `next` is the first step not done; `summary` says how many are done and what the next one needs ("the engine has 2 actions ready", "the model is asked (large call)", "you are asked", "waits on analysis plan"). `describeWorkflow` is the card's text; `modelSteps` lists what the cost preview prices. `populationFromObjective` reads "UK adults who bought a car" out of "Understand why UK adults who bought a car switch from Brand A to Brand B" (the noun, its modifiers, the relative clause up to the sentence's own verb); `constructName` names a side as a noun; `questionsMeasuring` finds the questions that already measure a name. In internal mode a model step keeps its sentence (so "once" can let it through) but asks the researcher.

**Tiers (`packages/ai`)** — `aiModelName("small" | "large")`; `completeJson` sends the tier's model. **The meter (`apps/studio/lib/aiEstimate.ts`)** — the estimate spec, carved out of `metering.ts` so it can be tested without a server: the tier's model is what is priced and reserved; under cost simulation the fake provider's "fake" report is priced at the configured model, but a real model name — the tier's — is kept.

**The route (`POST /api/copilot/workflow`)** — `project.read`; the sandbox against the fake provider sends its own definition. It gives the planner what only the server knows (fieldwork completes, the project's mode) and returns the workflow, the mode (project, effective, whether a request override applied), the model (configured, provider, small, large), the cost per model step and the total, and `runAvailable`. The planner runs in the Studio too, on the survey as it stands in the editor (the open proposal included), so a step applied is done at once; the route's facts are read when the tab opens, on refresh, when the mode changes, and when a model step appears that has not been priced.

**The Studio** — `useCopilot`: `executionMode` / `setExecutionMode`, `cloudOnce` / `setCloudOnce`, `workflow` (the planner on `working`), `workflowInfo`, `refreshWorkflow`, `showWorkflow` (the `workflow` interpretation), `runWorkflowStep` (an engine step → `local` as a proposal with `workflow: <step>` in its intent; an output step → `makeOutput`, which marks the document or the deck produced; a model step → `ask`); the internal-mode gate in `run` before any request leaves. `WorkflowTab` (`cp-tab-workflow`): the summary, the Internal / Cloud switch with the configured models, the cost line, the twelve steps with their marks, reasons, buttons (`wf-do-<step>`, `wf-ask-<step>`, the examples as chips that go to the input box) and what a blocked step waits on. `IntelligentView` routes the `workflow` kind and words the engine's fallback for internal mode.
