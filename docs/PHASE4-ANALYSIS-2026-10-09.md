**Source:** the Research Engine audit (`RESEARCH-ENGINE-AUDIT-2026-10-08.md`, section K, Phase 4), on top of Phases 1–3. The outcome it set: *the analysis layer is reachable by sentence and explains itself from the data.* Every change has a regression test and the tests were mutation-checked. ## What was built

**Corrections (`packages/analytics/src/posthoc.ts`)** — `adjustP(ps, method)`: Holm's step-down (monotone), Bonferroni (clamped), Benjamini–Hochberg (step-up); nulls stay out of the family. `pairwiseComparisons(ds, outcome, group, { method, nonparametric })`: Welch's t (or Mann–Whitney) on every pair, the pairs' p-values adjusted as one family, a summary naming the pairs that differ and which way. In `runPlan`, `applyCorrections` groups the planned findings by hypothesis (a finding serving two goes to the larger family; the rest are "the plan"), writes `evidence.adjusted` and reports per family how many held; `correctedVerdict` gives a supported verdict a corrected reading (mixed when some of its significant tests still hold, not supported when none does) — the raw verdict is untouched. A significant ANOVA / Kruskal–Wallis with three or more groups carries its pairwise result in the finding's evidence and detail. `briefText` says which findings do not hold once corrected and the correction summary.

**Data advice (`dataAdvice.ts`)** — `adviseAnalysis(def, ds)` runs the checks the method's rule of thumb implies, on the actual data: group sizes against 30, expected counts against 5 (on the table as the data fills it), skewness within groups, the variance ratio, the share at a scale's ends, missing answers, thin categories, cases per predictor. It names a recommended method only when a check calls for one, and `ok` is false whenever there is a warning or a recommendation. A run carries advice for the items that have any; `runPlan({ adapt: true })` runs the recommended test beside the planned one (marked `adaptedFrom`), serving the same hypothesis.

**Beyond the plan (`synthesis.ts`)** — `discoverSegments`: the outcomes (scale and numeric question variables, categorical ones in a dependent role, derived scores; never the system columns) by the cuts (the demographics and the plan's segments), skipping the pairs the plan already tests and columns with no spread; ANOVA or chi-square per pair, every p corrected as one family, kept only when significant with at least a moderate effect, largest effect first. `findAnomalies`: ceiling / floor at 60%, a category 90% chose (not a multi-select's option column), categories with fewer than five answers on a base of 100, extreme values on a continuous measure (more than ten distinct values), a third missing. `waveTrends`: the wave variable (a question named wave, else `_started_month`, else `_started_week`, needing two buckets of thirty) and each outcome across it, corrected. `synthesize` bundles the three with what was looked at and a one-line summary; `runPlan` carries it as `discoveries` unless `discover: false`.

**Data questions (`packages/engine/src/dataQuestion.ts`, `packages/analytics/src/dataQuery.ts`)** — the engine reads *which groups prefer / consider / are aware of X [by Y] [among Z]*, *what share / how many … chose X [in Qn]* (a yes/no question named by its wording gives its Yes; the question alone gives the count of answers), *what is the average / median X [by Y] [among Z]*, *how satisfied are men on average*, *does X differ by Y*, *are women more satisfied than men*, *which brand is most considered*, *what is the most common answer to Qn [by Y]* into a `DataQuery` — the variable, the option (resolved by its label, the verb deciding between questions that share it, "in Q5" settling it), the cut, the population as a parsed condition. A new Interpretation kind, `query`, hands it to the Studio; a question and an edit in one sentence still go to the model. `answerDataQuery` answers on a dataset: the share or count with a Wilson interval (and by a cut, with the chi-square), the mean with its interval (and by a cut, with the test), the comparison with the test, the effect and the pairs, which groups choose an option more than the rest (every demographic, each group against the rest, Holm-adjusted, small bases left out), the most common answer; a base below 30 is said, weighting is said, an empty population is said.

**The Studio** — `POST /api/copilot/ask`: a signed-in user with `analytics.read` asks on the survey's clean live data through the same dataset builder as the workspace (derived variables included); the sandbox, against the fake provider only, may send its own definition and rows (the browser suites' seam `window.__rescriptAnalyticsRows`), and without rows is told so (409). `useCopilot.askData` puts the query to the route and shows the answer as an engine answer with its sections, a *Read from the data* line (base, test, p, effect, caveats) and the planned finding on the same variables from the last run. The Findings tab shows the correction summary, each finding's adjusted p (and "does not hold once corrected"), a corrected verdict, the data advice with its recommendation, the discoveries by kind, and "Add … to the plan" for the first discovery whose variables are questions — through the engine, as a sentence. The run brief sent with a findings turn carries the new fields, and `briefText` prints them. Migration `0048_analytics_runs_phase4.sql` adds `corrections`, `advice` and `discoveries` to `analytics_runs`; `lib/analyticsRunStore.ts` inserts with them and, on a database without the columns, once more without — the run is kept either way and the page still shows what was computed.
