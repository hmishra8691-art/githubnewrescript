# Research Engine — Phase 7: Unrationed output, public documentation, the semantic tier, enforcement, consolidation (10-10-2026)

**Source:** the Research Engine audit (`RESEARCH-ENGINE-AUDIT-2026-10-08.md`): §F ①b (a semantic matcher over a synonym lexicon for low-confidence intents), §F ⑤ and §E "Validation" (research-level checks as blockers), §C.2 #1/#4 and §K Phase 1's output discipline (the answer must be complete, not cheap), and the consolidation pass the roadmap closes with — the whole of Phases 1–6 re-read against the code. Two asks from the brief that the audit did not list are here too: Intelligent mode **does not ration tokens** — the correct, complete answer is what matters — and ReScript Studio is **publicly documented** for the AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google's) that a researcher will ask "how do I program this in ReScript Studio?", as Qualtrics and Decipher are. Every change has a regression test and the tests were mutation-checked. Nothing existing was removed; the semantic tier runs only where the recognisers had nothing, the blockers only when the design is enforced, the docs are read-only public pages.

## What was built

**The budget (`apps/studio/lib/copilot/budget.ts`)** — `BudgetKind` = edit, review, ux, generate, plan, item, repair, coverage, narrative, summary. `maxOutputTokens(env)` reads `AI_MAX_OUTPUT_TOKENS` (bad → 32 000; floor 8 000; cap 1 000 000). `outputBudget(kind)` = `{ maxTokens: max(ceiling, expected[kind]), expectedTokens, continuations: 8, timeoutMs }`. The meter reserves the expected size (`meteredAi … maxTokens: expectedTokens`) so a wallet is not drained by a reservation it will not use; the call itself may use the ceiling. In `packages/ai`, `completeJson` keeps the known ceiling per `${base} ${model}` (`OUTPUT_CEILING`, `resetOutputCeilings()` for tests); a 400 that names a smaller limit lowers the call once and remembers it; a truncated answer after every continuation reports the limit it hit.

**The docs (`apps/studio/content/docs`, `lib/docs/markdown.ts`, `lib/docs/pages.ts`)** — `DOC_ORDER` is the navigation and `llms.txt`; `readDoc(slug)` accepts one path segment of `[a-z0-9-]`; `renderMarkdown` escapes every character and draws headings (with ids), paragraphs, fences, tables (escaped pipes), lists, quotes, rules; comments are dropped. Routes: `app/docs/page.tsx`, `app/docs/[slug]/page.tsx` (`DocShell` with `docs`, `docs-nav`, `docs-main`), `app/api/docs/[slug]/route.ts` (text/markdown, reached by the middleware rewrite of `/docs/<slug>.md`), `app/llms.txt`, `app/llms-full.txt`, `app/robots.ts`, `app/sitemap.ts`. `next.config.mjs` traces `content/docs` into the deploy. The generator (`scripts/lib/publicDocs.mjs`) builds question-types from the variant registry, logic-reference from the operator, function, piping and UX-API tables in the source, actions-reference from the action union.

**The semantic tier (`packages/engine/src/nlSemanticMatch.ts`)** — `semanticReading(def, text, ctx)` → `{ core, candidates, objects }`; a candidate is `{ intent, canonical, score, why }`. Slots: a question (`fuzzyQuestion`), a pair (split at the cue), a condition (`conditionInWords`: *under 25*, *at least 3*, *N or older*, *is not*, *said X to Q*, and/or), an option (quoted or *a X option*), a concept (only one the standard-measure library knows), a quoted text. `interpretRequest`'s `semantic(r)` verifies up to six candidates by reading each canonical at depth 1 and keeps those that produce actions, answers, queries or a no-op refusal; a model-with-fallback is kept as what the engine can offer.

**Blockers (`packages/engine/src/researchBlockers.ts`)** — `researchStrict(def)`, `researchBlockers(def)`, `newResearchBlockers(before, after)` (the codes present after and not before, so pre-existing gaps do not block unrelated edits). `applySurveyActions` refuses after the schema parse; `reviewSurvey` sets `blocks` and `blockers`; `set_research` carries `strict`; the editor's `rd-strict-box` patches it.

**Script scope (`packages/engine/src/scripts.ts`)** — `refs` is the set; a survey-scoped script runs on every occurrence; `only: "scoped"` still leaves the survey-wide `on_load` to the session open.

## Configuration

`AI_MAX_OUTPUT_TOKENS` — the provider's output ceiling (default 32 000). `AI_MODEL_SMALL` — the small tier. `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` — makes `robots.txt` point at the sitemap and `llms.txt` carry absolute links. All three documented in `.env.example`.

## Status of the roadmap after this phase

Phases 1–7 of the audit's §K are implemented and verified. The §D "critical" and "high" rows are all closed; of the "medium" rows, derived variables in the compute path, the clean dataset for automatic runs, post-hoc corrections, factor scores, chart selection by purpose, the premium deck, output routing and the model tiers are done; "findings synthesis beyond the plan" (segment discovery, anomalies, wave trends) is done. Of the "low" rows, the research ontology exists as the standard-measure library (purchase intent, satisfaction, likelihood to recommend, awareness, importance, loyalty, trust, usage frequency, price perception) the engine uses without a model; cross-project memory and a project "brief" entity are not built, and the §J architecture remains conceptual as the audit intended.
