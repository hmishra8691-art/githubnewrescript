## What was built

**Chart choice (`packages/analytics/src/chartPurpose.ts`)** — `purposeOf(def, result)` reads why a result is shown: a crosstab compares, a test shows a difference with its uncertainty, a correlation or model relates, a descriptive spreads, NPS is a number to watch, ranking and MaxDiff rank, segmentation profiles, time-like categories are a trend whatever the kind. `chooseChart(def, result, { purpose?, audience? })` starts from `recommendCharts` (the shape), lifts the types that serve the purpose (bars for a comparison, stacked-100 and donuts for a composition, the histogram for a distribution, the line for a trend, the scatter for a relationship, ranked bars for a ranking, the KPI card, the interval chart or the bars for significance) and lifts or lowers families for the audience (an executive loses the statistical and significance families and gains bars and KPIs; a researcher gains the significance family). A chart the data cannot draw is never a candidate; the reason names the purpose and the audience with their weights.

**The deck (`deck.ts`, `export/deck.ts`)** — `deckFromRun(def, run, { audience, client, maxFindings, narrative, results })` tells the story: the title; the summary (objective, verdicts, the strongest findings, what was found beyond the plan); a key finding per significant planned finding, strongest first, with the chart chosen for the audience, the evidence line (test, p, effect, n, the adjusted p) and the so-what (the hypothesis it is evidence for and its verdict; a strong or moderate effect "worth acting on", a small one "real, but modest"; a corrected-away finding "read with caution"); who differs (the groups highest first, the pairs that differ); statistical highlights (the effect as a callout); beyond the plan (the discoveries not already shown, the anomalies as caveats); implications (each verdict against the design); recommendations (lead with the strongest finding, treat a corrected-away one with caution, re-run an inconclusive one, plan the test an untested one lacks, add a discovery to the plan, re-run as the data advises); method; caveats. `buildDeckPptx` draws it with one builder per slide type on `DECK_STYLE` (display 36 / title 22 / headline 17 / body 12 / caption 9 / statistic 40), the theme's palette and fonts, a kicker per slide, the significance badge, cards with a soft shadow, native editable charts through the existing `drawAnalysisVisual`.

**The documents (`export/docx.ts`)** — both on the `docx` library the questionnaire export uses, with the same page and type conventions. The proposal needs no data: it is written from the survey and its research design, with the engine's readings (`structuredHypotheses`, `explainPlan`, `planSampleSize`, `expectedSample`, `inferRole`). The findings report is written from a run through the same `deckFromRun` story, with the groups and the chart data as tables.

**Output by sentence (`packages/engine/src/nlIntent.ts`, the `output` kind)** — the recogniser reads the verb, the modifiers (client-ready, final, full, executive, findings …), the noun (proposal / research design document; presentation / deck / slides / PowerPoint; findings report / report as a Word document / Word document of the findings) and the tail ("for the board" is an executive edition, "for the analysts" a researcher's, "for Acme Ltd" the client, "for this survey" nothing); a tail it cannot read, an edit in the same sentence, an executive summary in words or a report to draft in the analytics workspace stay the model's. A bare survey is refused with what to record first.

**The Studio** — `POST /api/copilot/output` (`project.read` for the proposal, `analytics.export` for the findings outputs; the sandbox against the fake provider sends its own definition, rows and a scripted narrative): the proposal from the design with the project's client and fieldwork dates; the findings outputs from a fresh `runPlan` on the clean live data; the narrative from the model (metered, `copilot_narrative`) through `gateNarrative`; the file as the response with `content-disposition`, what it holds and what the gate kept in headers. `useCopilot.makeOutput` shows the file on the card (`cp-output`: name, size, a download link, the summary, the narrative gate's verdict); `IntelligentView` routes the `output` kind there; the Findings tab's *Documents* section offers the proposal, the findings report, the findings deck and the executive deck as sentences the engine reads.
